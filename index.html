Meet the Coaches

Two experts.
One shared goal.

Both coaches compete actively in local and regional tournaments — meaning the instruction you get is current, practical, and proven on the course. Not just in a textbook.

Head Coach & Academy Founder
Yaser García
PGA PGM TPI Level 2 Capto Putting Certified Club Fitting Expert
"No two golfers are the same. I work closely with you to understand your strengths and areas for improvement, and develop a coaching plan tailored to your unique needs and goals."

Yaser's methodology covers all three pillars: physical conditioning, mental preparation, and technical skills training working together as one system. With 13+ years playing competitive golf, he dedicated his career to sharing the game — bringing swing mechanics, Capto putting mastery, TPI fitness, and club fitting expertise under one roof.

Instructor & Club Fitting Specialist
Memo Vega
PGA PGM Level 3 Candidate ADM Certified Teaching & Coaching
"I pride myself on providing a personalized approach to each of my students — understanding the cause-and-effect relationship between actions and outcomes on the course."

Memo's teaching philosophy is rooted in the mental side of the game. He uses communication and visualization techniques to make improvement stick between sessions, not just on the lesson mat. His patient, step-by-step approach has produced measurable results for everyone from beginners to competitive high school athletes.
