Questions

Good to
know.

Everything you need before your first session. Still wondering about something? Just give us a call.

Do I need my own clubs?

Not at all. We have clubs on hand so you can show up empty-handed and start swinging. That said, if you have your own set, bring it — practicing with the clubs you actually play makes your lesson data and adjustments far more useful.

How long is a lesson?

Private, junior, and high-school lessons run a full 60 minutes. Putting sessions in our bundle are 30 minutes. Every minute is one-on-one with your coach — there's no shared range time or waiting on a tee.

I'm a complete beginner — is this for me?

Absolutely. We coach every skill level, from first-time beginners who've never held a club to competitive high-school athletes. Your coach meets you exactly where you are and builds from there — no judgment, no assumptions.

Is it really fully indoor? What about the El Paso heat or wind?

Yes — we're 100% indoor and climate-controlled year-round. No heat, no wind, no rain delays, and no tee times to chase. You get consistent conditions every visit, which is exactly what makes measurable improvement possible.

What ages do you teach? Do you have junior programs?

We work with juniors through adults. There are dedicated Junior and High School lesson programs built around patient, structured instruction for young golfers — many families book lessons for both a parent and their kids.

What technology do you use during a lesson?

Every session is backed by launch-monitor data (ball speed, spin, launch angle, carry) and multi-angle video analysis. We also offer Capto putting analysis and TPI fitness assessment. You don't just feel the change — you see it in the numbers.

How do I book, and what if I need to reschedule?

Book anytime through our online scheduler, or call (915) 449-3613. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a coach is free. Need to move a session? Just give us a call or message and we'll find a new time.
