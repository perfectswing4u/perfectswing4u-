The Process

Simple from
the start.

Whether you've never held a club or you're shaving strokes off a single-digit handicap, the path forward is the same: book, show up, improve.

01
Book Your Session
Reserve online in under two minutes through our booking system. Choose your coach, pick your service, and pick a time that works. Walk-ins are always welcome when coaches are available.
02
Get on the Monitor
Your first session starts with a data baseline — ball speed, launch angle, spin rate, smash factor. Your coach identifies the one or two things that will unlock the most improvement for your specific swing.
03
Track Real Progress
Every session produces numbers you can measure against. You'll leave with specific drills, a clear sense of what's improving, and a plan for your next session. The data doesn't lie — and neither does the scoreboard.

Meet the Coaches

Two experts.
One shared goal.

Both coaches compete actively in local and regional tournaments — meaning the instruction you get is current, practical, and proven on the course. Not just in a textbook.

Head Coach & Academy Founder
Yaser
García
PGA PGM TPI Level 2 Capto Putting Certified Club Fitting Expert
"No two golfers are the same. I work closely with you to understand your strengths and areas for improvement, and develop a coaching plan tailored to your unique needs and goals."

Yaser's methodology covers all three pillars of the game: physical conditioning, mental preparation, and technical skills training working together as one system. With 13+ years playing competitive golf, he got so passionate about the game that he dedicated his career to sharing it with others. He brings swing mechanics, putting mastery (Capto certified), golf fitness (TPI Level 2), and club fitting expertise all under one roof.

Instructor & Club Fitting Specialist
Memo
Vega
PGA PGM Level 3 Candidate ADM Certified Teaching & Coaching
"I pride myself on providing a personalized approach to each of my students — understanding the cause-and-effect relationship between actions and outcomes on the course."

Memo's teaching philosophy is rooted in the mental side of the game. He uses effective communication and visualization techniques to make improvement stick between sessions, not just on the lesson mat. His patient, step-by-step approach has produced measurable results for students ranging from absolute beginners to competitive high school athletes — often in a single lesson.
